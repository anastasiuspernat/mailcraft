- Mail of every account in one place: with two accounts or more, the folder pane starts with All Inboxes (each account's Inbox under it), VIPs, Flagged, All Drafts and All Sent. The smart Inbox's tabs and Priority work over All Inboxes. Right-click a message › Add Sender to VIPs: their mail from every account is listed under VIPs, marked with a star, and kept in Primary. On macOS the Dock icon shows the unread mail of all Inboxes. File › Options can hide them.
