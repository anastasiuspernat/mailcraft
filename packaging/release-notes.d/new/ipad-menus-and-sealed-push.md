- iPad: MailCraft's menus in the iPadOS menu bar (the pointer at the top of the screen, or ⌘ held), the Mac's File, Edit, View, Folder, Message and Help with their keys, and Settings… (⌘,) for File › Options; the ribbon is one row of commands without tabs. Cut, Copy and Paste work with other apps.
- iPad and MailCraft relay: the iPad hands the relay its own accounts (sealed, on the home network) and the relay notifies each iPad of its own accounts alone; an account removed on the iPad is dropped there with its sign-in. Pairing proves the code without sending it (SPAKE2), notifications show the sender and the subject sealed for the iPad alone (Apple carries "New mail"), the relay renews IDLE without reconnecting, waits an hour after a refused sign-in, and `mailcraft-relay devices` and `unpair` list and forget iPads. An iPad paired by the earlier version pairs again.
- Summaries are made by themselves only while File › Options › "Show summaries in the message list" is on (any message of 12 words or more), and otherwise only when you press Summarize; the word count and "as it arrives" settings are gone.
