- The smart Inbox: a language model on this computer sorts the Inbox into Primary, Transactions, Updates and Promotions, shown as tabs above it with their unread counts, then All Mail for the whole Inbox, on one line that scrolls sideways in a narrow window, and writes a short summary, with what is asked and by when, at the top of a long message or a conversation. It is off until a model file (GGUF) is chosen in File › Options; mail and summaries never leave the computer, the model runs in a process of its own, and on an Intel Mac with a Radeon card it uses the graphics card through Vulkan, after a self-test, else the processor. Time-sensitive mail comes first in Primary under Priority, unread mail shows its summary in the message list, and in the other parts each sender's mail folds into one row with "3 more from …". With an embedding model chosen, search also finds mail by meaning ("flight change" finds "Your booking was rebooked"), marked By meaning, on this computer alone.
